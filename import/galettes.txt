GALETTES (buckwheat, crispy) — 1 egg batch (about 4–5 galettes)

INGREDIENTS
1 large egg
270 ml water
90 g buckwheat flour
3 g fine salt
3–5 g unsalted butter (or a thin film of neutral oil) for the pan, per galette

METHOD
1) Whisk 90 g buckwheat flour with 3 g fine salt.
2) Whisk in about 170 ml water to make a thick paste, smoothing out lumps.
3) Whisk in the remaining 100 ml water until the batter is fluid and spreads easily.
4) Whisk in 1 large egg until fully combined.
5) Press 1 sheet plastic film directly on the batter surface.
6) Rest 2–4 hours in the fridge, or 12 hours in the fridge for maximum hydration.
7) Whisk the batter briefly before cooking.
8) Heat a pan over medium-high heat until a drop of water sizzles instantly.
9) Grease the pan with 3–5 g unsalted butter (or a very thin film of neutral oil).
10) Pour a thin layer of batter, swirl fast, and cook 70–110 seconds until the surface looks dry and the edges are well browned.
11) Flip, then cook 50–90 seconds until browned with spots and the galette feels drier and crisper.
12) For extra crispiness, flip once more for 15–30 seconds to dry the first side again.
13) If needed: whisk in 10–30 ml water to thin; or whisk in 5–10 g buckwheat flour to strengthen.
