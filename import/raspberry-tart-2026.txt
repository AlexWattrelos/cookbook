French Raspberry Tart (Tarte aux Framboises)

Servings: 8 (24 cm tart)

SHOPPING LIST

Veggies:
- Fresh raspberries — 500 g

Eggs:
- Eggs — 4

Fridge:
- Unsalted butter — 170 g
- Whole milk — 375 ml

Anything else:
- Plain flour — 250 g
- Almond flour (ground almonds) — 50 g
- Icing sugar — 90 g
- Caster sugar — 75 g
- Cornstarch — 35 g
- Ground Bourbon vanilla — ½ tsp
- Seedless raspberry jam or red currant jelly — 60 g
- Shelled unsalted pistachios — 20 g (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS

Pâte sucrée
1. Put 250 g flour, 90 g icing sugar, 50 g almond flour, a pinch of salt and 140 g cold cubed butter in the TM7. TM7: 10 sec / speed 6, until sandy.
2. Stop, add 1 egg. TM7: 15 sec / speed 5, just until it clumps. Do not overmix.
3. Tip out, press into a flat disc, wrap, chill in the fridge 1 h.

Pastry cream
4. Wash the bowl. Add 375 ml whole milk, 3 egg yolks, 75 g caster sugar, 35 g cornstarch and ½ tsp ground Bourbon vanilla. TM7: 6–7 min / 90°C / speed 4, until thick and glossy.
5. Stop, add 30 g butter. TM7: 20 sec / speed 5, until glossy.
6. Transfer to a dish, press cling film flat onto the surface, cool, then chill in the fridge at least 2 h.

Shell
7. Roll the chilled dough between two sheets of parchment to ~2 mm. Line a 24 cm tart tin, press into the edges, trim. Freeze 20 min.
8. Heat oven to 200°C conventional / 180°C fan (convection). Line the shell with parchment and fill with baking beans. Blind bake 15 min.
9. Remove the parchment and beans. Bake the empty shell 10–12 min more, until deep golden brown and crisp. If the rim colours too fast, loosely cover it with foil.
10. Brush the inside of the hot shell with a little egg white and bake 2 min more to seal. Cool completely in the tin to room temperature.

Assemble
11. No more than 3 hours before serving, so the shell stays crisp: whisk the cold pastry cream smooth (by hand, or TM7 20 sec / speed 4). Spread evenly into the cooled shell and level the top.
12. Arrange 500 g raspberries upright in tight concentric circles, hollow-side down.
13. Warm 60 g seedless raspberry jam with 1 tbsp water until fluid (microwave or small pan). Brush a thin coat over the raspberries.

Serve chilled.

OPTIONAL: Scatter 20 g crushed pistachios around the rim for color and crunch.
