Thermomix potato purée (serves ~4)

Ingredients
- 1 kg floury potatoes (Agria, Bintje, Maris Piper), peeled and halved
- 220–300 g cold butter, cubed (start with 220 g)
- 250–300 ml hot milk (or 200 ml milk + 50–100 ml hot cream)
- fine salt, white pepper
- 500 g water (for steaming)

Method
1. Steam - Add 500 g water to bowl. Potatoes in Varoma. Varoma / 25–30 min / speed 1, until just tender.
2. Dry - Discard water. Potatoes into empty bowl. 100 °C / 1 min / reverse / speed 1, measuring cup off.
3. Mash (butterfly) - Insert butterfly. Add ½ tsp salt + half the butter. Reverse / speed 3 / 15–20 sec; scrape.
4. Add remaining butter. Reverse / speed 2.5–3 / 10–15 sec, just until smooth (tiny flecks ok).
5. Loosen - With butterfly in, add hot milk/cream a little at a time. 70 °C / reverse / speed 2 / 10–15 sec; check. Repeat to a silky, slow-flowing purée. Season with salt and white pepper.
6. Hold (optional) - 70 °C for up to 20 min, butterfly in. Stir 5–6 sec / reverse / speed 2 every few minutes. Do not overwork.
