Asparagus with Hollandaise Sauce, Ham and Baby Potatoes

Servings: 2

SHOPPING LIST

Veggies:
- 400 g green asparagus
- 300 g baby potatoes
- 1 lemon

Fridge:
- 200 g cooked ham (thick-sliced)
- 150 g butter (unsalted)
- 2 egg yolks

Anything else:
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Boil 300 g baby potatoes in salted water until tender, about 15–20 min depending on size. Drain and keep warm.
2. Snap woody ends off 400 g green asparagus.
3. TM7 — melt butter: Add 150 g butter, 4 min / 75°C / speed 1. Pour melted butter into a jug or small bowl.
4. TM7 — start Hollandaise: Insert butterfly whisk. Add 2 egg yolks, 1.5 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, a pinch of salt, and a pinch of white pepper. Set 30s / 60°C / speed 3.
5. Pour in roughly a third of the melted butter. Close lid and set 1 min / 60°C / speed 3.
6. Pour in the rest of the butter. Set 3 min / 80°C / speed 3.
7. Hollandaise should be thick and creamy. Taste and adjust salt and lemon.
8. While Hollandaise runs, cook asparagus: heat a large pan over high heat with a drizzle of oil. Add 400 g asparagus, season with salt, and sear 4–5 min, turning occasionally, until tender-crisp with light char. Finish with black pepper off the heat.
9. Serve 200 g cooked ham, asparagus and baby potatoes with Hollandaise spooned over.
