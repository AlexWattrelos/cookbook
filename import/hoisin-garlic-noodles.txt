RECIPE: HOISIN GARLIC NOODLES (SERVES 2)

Ingredients
- 250 g dried wheat or egg noodles
- 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice vinegar or lime juice
- 1 tsp sugar or honey
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 tsp freshly grated ginger (optional)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (optional)
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced
- 1 small handful fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds (optional)

Steps
1. Cook noodles
   - Boil a large pot of salted water.
   - Cook 250 g noodles until just tender.
   - Drain and keep 60 ml (¼ cup) cooking water aside.

2. Mix sauce
   - In a bowl, whisk together: 4 tbsp hoisin sauce, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp rice vinegar or lime juice, 1 tsp sugar or honey, 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (if using), 60 ml (¼ cup) noodle cooking water.

3. Fry aromatics
   - Heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in a large pan over medium heat.
   - Add 4 chopped garlic cloves and 1 tsp grated ginger.
   - Cook 30–60 seconds, just until fragrant (don't brown too much).

4. Combine
   - Add cooked noodles to the pan.
   - Pour in the sauce.
   - Toss over medium heat for 1–2 minutes until glossy and well coated.
   - Taste and adjust with salt and black pepper.

5. Finish and serve
   - Turn off heat. Stir in most of the sliced scallions and cilantro.
   - Plate the noodles and top with remaining scallions, cilantro, and 1 tbsp sesame seeds if using.
   - Serve hot.
