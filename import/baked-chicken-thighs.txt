RECIPE: BAKED CHICKEN THIGHS (SERVES 3–4)

Ingredients
- 1 kg bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped or grated
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp honey (optional, for a touch of sweetness)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano or thyme
- 1 tbsp butter, cut into small pieces (optional, for richness)
- 1 small handful fresh parsley, chopped (optional, for serving)

Steps
1. Heat oven
   - Heat oven to 220°C.
   - Line a baking tray with parchment or foil.

2. Make marinade
   - In a bowl, mix: 3 cloves chopped garlic, 2 tbsp Dijon mustard, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tbsp honey (if using), 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp dried oregano or thyme.

3. Season chicken
   - Pat 1 kg chicken thighs dry.
   - Rub with salt, black pepper, and a little neutral oil.
   - Coat all over with the marinade (use your hands to get under the skin if you like).
   - Arrange thighs skin-side up on the tray.
   - Dot with 1 tbsp butter pieces on top (if using).

4. Bake
   - Bake 30–40 minutes, until skin is browned and crisp and juices run clear (internal temperature about 75°C).
   - If you want extra crisp skin, switch to grill/broil for the last 2–3 minutes.

5. Serve
   - Rest 5 minutes.
   - Sprinkle with chopped parsley.
   - Serve with rice, potatoes, or a simple salad.
