Saucisson Vaudois with Leek and Potato Gratin

Servings: 4

SHOPPING LIST

Veggies:
- 600 g leeks (about 3 large)
- 2 cloves garlic
- 800 g waxy potatoes

Fridge:
- 400 ml heavy cream
- 80 g Gruyère (grated)
- 20 g butter

Meat / Fish:
- 1 saucisson vaudois (~400-500 g)

Anything else:
- Nutmeg (whole or ground)
- Whole-grain mustard (for serving)

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Preheat oven to 160°C fan (convection).
2. Trim and wash 600 g leeks, slice into 5 mm rounds (white and light-green parts). Melt 20 g butter in a large pan over medium heat, add the leeks, season with salt, sweat 8-10 min until soft. Set aside.
3. While the leeks cook, peel 800 g potatoes and slice 3 mm thin (mandoline or knife). Thinly slice 2 cloves garlic.
4. Lightly butter a 25-28 cm baking dish. Layer: a third of the potatoes, salt and pepper, half the garlic and half the leeks. Repeat: another third of potatoes, salt and pepper, remaining garlic and leeks. Finish with the last third of potatoes, salt and pepper.
5. Season 400 ml cream with salt, pepper, and a grating of nutmeg. Pour evenly over the gratin. Press the layers down with your hand or a spatula so the cream rises up and nearly covers the top potatoes.
6. Scatter 80 g Gruyère over the top. Cover with foil. Bake covered 45 min.
7. After 30 min of baking, place the saucisson vaudois in a pot, cover with cold water. Bring to a bare simmer — never boil or the casing will burst. Poach 45 min.
8. After 45 min of baking, remove foil from the gratin. Set oven to 180°C. Bake another 20-25 min until golden and bubbling and a knife slides easily through the potatoes.
9. Rest the gratin 10 min. Slice saucisson into 1 cm rounds, serve alongside with whole-grain mustard.
