DISHOOM CHICKEN RUBY (SERVES 4)

Shopping list

Veggies:
- Garlic: 12 cloves (20 cloves for 2x sauce)
- Fresh ginger: 30 g (50 g for 2x sauce)
- Lime: 1
- Fresh dill fronds

Fridge items:
- Full-fat Greek yoghurt: 75 g
- Double cream: 130 ml (210 ml for 2x sauce)
- Unsalted butter: 100 g

Meat / Fish:
- Boneless, skinless chicken thighs: 700 g

Canned food:
- Tinned crushed tomatoes: 800 g (1600 g for 2x sauce)

Anything else:
- Neutral oil (high-oleic rapeseed / avocado): 125 ml (210 ml for 2x sauce)
- Bay leaves: 2
- Green cardamom pods: 6
- Black cardamom pods: 2
- Cinnamon stick: 1
- Deggi Mirch chilli powder
- Garam masala
- Ground cumin
- Kasoori methi
- Sugar: 20 g
- Runny honey: 1 tbsp

MARINATE THE CHICKEN (6–24H AHEAD)
1. In a mixing bowl, add 10 g fresh ginger, 20 g garlic, 2 tsp fine sea salt, 1 tsp deggi mirch chilli powder, 1½ tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp garam masala, 2 tsp lime juice, 2 tsp vegetable oil, 75 g Greek yoghurt. Blitz until smooth
2. Toss 700 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs (4 cm chunks) with the marinade in a bowl, cover, and refrigerate 6–24 h.

PREP THE TOMATOES
- Add 800 g chopped tomatoes to the clean mixing bowl. Blend 10 sec/speed 8 until smooth. Transfer to a separate bowl and set aside.

MAKHANI
1. Add 175 ml vegetable oil and 15 g thinly sliced garlic to the mixing bowl. Browning mode 6 min (gentle, splash guard on) until pale-gold; lift out the garlic chips and reserve. Leave the oil in the bowl.
2. Add 2 bay leaves, 6 green cardamom pods, 2 black cardamom pods, 1 cinnamon stick to the hot oil. Cook 2 min/120°C/Reverse/speed 1.
3. Discard spices.
4. Add remaining garlic (about 20 g) and 20 g fresh ginger (roughly chopped). Chop 5 sec/speed 7; scrape down. Sauté 4 min/120°C/Reverse/speed 1.
5. Add the reserved tomato purée, 2 tsp fine sea salt, 1½ tsp deggi mirch chilli powder. Cook Steaming mode 25–30 min/Reverse/speed 1 (MC off, basket on lid) until reduced by about half and glossy.
6. Add 30 g butter. Cook 3 min/100°C/Reverse/speed 1.
7. Add 1 tsp garam masala, 20 g sugar, 1 tbsp runny honey, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp crushed kasoori methi, ½ tsp chopped dill, and the reserved garlic chips. Cook 12–15 min/100°C/Reverse/speed 1.
8. Add 80 ml double cream. Stir 1 min/90°C/Reverse/speed 1. Keep warm at 70°C/Reverse/speed 1 while you cook the chicken.

GRILL THE CHICKEN
Heat grill/broiler to max. Arrange marinated chicken on a rack over a tray with something below to collect the marinade falling; brush with 20 g melted unsalted butter. Grill 8–10 min, turning once, until lightly charred and cooked through.

FINISH THE CURRY
Add grilled chicken and 50 ml double cream to the sauce in the mixing bowl. Cook 10 min/80°C/Reverse/Soft so the chicken stays in chunks.

GARNISH & SERVE
Rest 2–3 min. Adjust salt if needed. Garnish with ginger matchsticks, chopped coriander, 1 tbsp pomegranate seeds. Serve with steamed basmati rice or naan.

NOTES
If making the sauce ahead, chill it without the cream; add 80 ml cream when reheating, then finish with the final 50 ml at Step 8. It can be kept frozen for up to 3 months.
