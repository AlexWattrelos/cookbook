Cheese Soufflé (serves 5-6 as starter, 2 as main)

Gear
- 6 ramekins or one 1 L soufflé dish
- Pastry brush, spatula
- Oven preheated 200 °C (no fan), rack lower–middle

Ingredients
- Soft butter, for greasing
- ~2 tbsp finely grated Parmesan (for coating ramekins)
- 40 g butter
- 40 g flour
- 1 tsp (5 g) cornstarch
- 300 ml whole milk
- ½ tsp fine salt + a pinch more to taste
- ¼ tsp white pepper
- Tiny pinch nutmeg
- 130 g Gruyère or Comté, in chunks (to grate)
- 4 large eggs, separated
- ½ tsp Dijon mustard (optional)
- ½ tsp lemon juice

0) Prep
1. Heat oven to 200 °C (no fan).
2. Separate 4 eggs: yolks in a cup; whites in a clean, grease-free bowl. Chill whites 10 minutes (optional, helps whipping).

1) Grate the cheese (TM7)
- Add 130 g cheese chunks to the TM7. 8 sec / Speed 8 → scrape down → 2–3 sec / Speed 8 if needed. Tip out and reserve.

2) Whip the egg whites
In a mixing bowl
1. Add 4 egg whites + ½ tsp lemon juice in a medium/large mixing bowl
2. Whip until soft-firm peaks (glossy, tips just curl)

3) Make the sturdy cheese base (TM7)
1. Add 40 g butter → 2 min / 100 °C / Speed 1 (melt).
2. Add 40 g flour + 1 tsp cornstarch → 2 min / 100 °C / Speed 2 (cook roux, no color).
3. Add 300 ml milk, ½ tsp salt, white pepper, nutmeg → 5 min / 100 °C / Speed 3 (thick like yogurt).
4. During that time, grease ramekins with upward strokes of butter; dust with Parmesan.
5. Add grated cheese and ½ tsp Dijon (optional) → 30–40 sec / 90 °C / Speed 3 (smooth).
6. 2 min / Speed 2 / no heat (MC off) to vent steam.
7. Add egg yolks → 15–20 sec / Speed 3 just to combine. Taste; season boldly (it mutes when baked).

4) Fold & fill (by hand)
1. Scrape the warm cheese base into a large empty bowl
2. Lighten: briskly mix in ~¼ of the whites.
3. Fold the rest with a spatula: scoop under, lift, ¼ turn—stop while a few pale streaks remain.
4. Spoon into ramekins to 5 mm below the rim (or the 1 L dish).
5. Chimney groove: run your thumb around the inside rim to create a shallow channel—helps an even "top hat" rise.

5) Bake
- Ramekins: 14–16 min (creamy center) or 17 min (more set).
- 1 L dish: 25–30 min.
Do not open the oven until the end. Done when well risen, browned, and a skewer inserted just off-center shows a slightly custardy smear.

Serve
Crack the top with a spoon, serve immediately. A sharp green salad is perfect.

TM7 success tips (tiny things that matter)
- Whites: Butterfly, Speed 3.5, MC off. Over-stiff whites make folding harder and rise lower.
- Zero grease: Any fat in the bowl kills volume. If unsure, wipe with a little lemon juice then dry.
- Cool base to warm before adding yolks/whites to avoid deflation.
- Don't fold in the Thermomix — always by hand.
- Cornstarch in the roux = extra structure and stability.
- No fan setting bakes more evenly for soufflé.

Optional twists (same timings)
- Chive: After step 3.6, add 2 tbsp snipped chives; fold in by hand during step 4.
- Blue: Swap ⅓ of the cheese for mild blue (add in step 3.4).
- Pancetta: Sauté finely diced pancetta on the stove, pat dry, fold in at step 4.
