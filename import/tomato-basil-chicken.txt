RECIPE: TOMATO BASIL CHICKEN (SERVES 2–3)

Ingredients
- 500 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into bite-size pieces
- 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 small onion or 2 shallots, finely chopped
- 400 g canned crushed tomatoes (or 400 g ripe fresh tomatoes, chopped)
- 60 ml (¼ cup) chicken stock or water
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar or red wine vinegar
- 10–15 fresh basil leaves, torn
- 30 g grated Parmesan or Pecorino (optional)
- 30 ml (2 tbsp) heavy cream or mascarpone (optional, for richness)

Steps
1. Brown chicken
   - Heat a little oil in a large pan over medium-high.
   - Season chicken with salt and pepper.
   - Sear until lightly browned, 4–6 minutes total. Transfer to a plate.

2. Cook aromatics
   - Lower heat to medium.
   - Add a bit more oil if needed.
   - Cook onion and garlic until soft and lightly golden, 3–5 minutes.

3. Make sauce
   - Add tomatoes, 60 ml stock, and 1 tbsp vinegar.
   - Simmer about 5 minutes.

4. Finish chicken
   - Return chicken and any juices to the pan.
   - Simmer gently 8–10 minutes, until cooked through and sauce thickened.
   - Stir in cream (if using) and most of the basil.
   - Taste and adjust with salt, pepper, or a pinch of sugar if too acidic.

5. Serve
   - Top with Parmesan and remaining basil.
   - Serve with pasta, rice, or bread.
