ULTIMATE LASAGNA (23×33 cm tray)

Servings: 10–12

Veggies:
- 1 large onion (~200 g)
- 1 large carrot (~150 g)
- 2 celery stalks (~150 g)
- 5 garlic cloves (~20 g)
- 15 g basil or parsley (optional, for serving)

Fridge items:
- 110 g butter
- 2 L whole milk
- 240 g Parmesan (block)
- 300 g low-moisture mozzarella

Meat / Fish:
- 1 marrow bone, lengthwise cut (approx 250–350 g bone-in; yields ~60–120 g marrow)
- 120 g pancetta
- 700 g ground beef (~20% fat)
- 350 g ground pork (or sausage meat)

Pasta / Rice / Quinoa / Grains:
- 250 g lasagna sheets (fresh OR dried)

Canned food:
- 1200 g crushed tomatoes
- 30 g tomato paste (2 tbsp)
- 500 ml stock (beef or chicken)

Anything else:
- 250 ml dry red wine (or dry white)
- 100 g all-purpose flour
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme (or 1 tsp Italian seasoning)
- Ground nutmeg (optional)

1) OVEN + DISH
- Heat the oven to 140°C conventional / 120°C fan (convection).
- Grease a 23×33 cm baking dish with 10 g butter.

2) TM7: CHOP VEGETABLES + GARLIC (SET ASIDE)
- TM7: Add 200 g onion, then chop 4 sec / speed 4. Scrape down.
- TM7: Add 150 g carrot + 150 g celery, then chop 4 sec / speed 4. Scrape down.
- Transfer the chopped onion/carrot/celery to a bowl.
- TM7: Add 20 g garlic cloves, then chop 2 sec / speed 7. Transfer to a small bowl.

3) SAUCE
- In a large heavy pot over medium-low heat, add 1 marrow bone (lengthwise cut, marrow-side up) and cook 6–10 min until marrow softens and fat starts rendering.
- Scoop out 60–120 g marrow into the pot (leave the bone behind), mash it into the fat, then let it sizzle 1 min.
- Add the chopped onion/carrot/celery (all from step 2) + 1 pinch salt, then cook 12–18 min over low heat until translucent and sweet (no browning).
- Scrape the softened vegetables into a bowl and reserve.
- In the same pot over medium heat, cook 120 g pancetta for 5–7 min until browned and fat renders.
- Add 700 g ground beef, spread it out, and leave it undisturbed 2–3 min to brown.
- Add 350 g ground pork, then cook 6–10 min total, breaking into chunks and letting it re-brown.
- Add the reserved vegetables, then cook 3–5 min, scraping up browned bits.
- Add 20 g chopped garlic, then cook 30–60 sec until fragrant.
- Add 30 g tomato paste (2 tbsp), then cook 2 min until it darkens slightly.
- Add 250 ml dry wine, then simmer 3–5 min until reduced by about half.
- Add 1200 g crushed tomatoes + 2 bay leaves + 1 tsp dried oregano + 1/2 tsp dried thyme + 1 Parmesan rind (optional), then bring to a gentle simmer.
- Add 250 ml stock, then simmer 60–120 min, partially uncovered, stirring occasionally.
- Add up to 250 ml additional stock only if the sauce becomes too thick before the flavours develop.
- During the last 20–30 min, add 250 ml whole milk, then simmer until thick and rich.
- Remove 2 bay leaves + 1 Parmesan rind (if used).

4) TM7: WHITE SAUCE
- TM7 (clean, dry bowl): Add 100 g butter, then melt 3 min / 100°C / speed 1.
- TM7: Add 100 g all-purpose flour, then cook 2 min / 100°C / speed 1.
- TM7: Stop → open lid → add 1700 ml whole milk + 1 pinch ground nutmeg (optional) → close lid.
- TM7: Cook 12–14 min / 90°C / speed 4.
- TM7: Stop → open lid → add 200 g Parmesan (cut into 2 cm chunks) → close lid.
- TM7: Blend 10 sec / speed 10.
- TM7: Cook 1 min / 90°C / speed 3.

5) PASTA
- If using 250 g fresh lasagna sheets, use as-is.
- If using 250 g dried lasagna sheets, blanch in salted boiling water 4–6 min until flexible but still very al dente, then lay flat.

6) TOPPING
- Hand-grate 40 g Parmesan very finely into a fluffy pile and keep it loose.

7) ASSEMBLE
- Spread 200 g sauce in the bottom of the dish.
- Add 1 layer lasagna sheets.
- Repeat 3–4 times: add 350 g sauce + 300 g white sauce + 75 g mozzarella, then add 1 layer lasagna sheets.
- Add 1 final layer lasagna sheets.
- Spread 500 g white sauce, then scatter 75 g mozzarella.

8) BAKE + GRILL + REST
- Bake 30 min at 140°C conventional / 120°C fan (convection).
- Switch the oven to grill/broiler on high and move the dish to an upper rack.
- Grill/broil 2–6 min until deeply golden and bubbling (watch constantly).
- Scatter 40 g hand-grated Parmesan over the top.
- Rest 25–40 min before slicing.
- Serve with 15 g basil or parsley (optional).
