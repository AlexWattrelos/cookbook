Tiramisu

Servings: 8

SHOPPING LIST

Eggs:
- 4 eggs (use 4 yolks + 2 whites)

Fridge items:
- 500 g mascarpone
- 200 ml heavy cream (Vollrahm, 35% fat)

Anything else:
- 200 g small ladyfinger biscuits (savoiardi) — about 30 pieces
- 100 g sugar
- 240 ml strong espresso, cooled
- 3 tbsp Marsala or Amaretto
- 30 g unsweetened cocoa powder

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Brew 240 ml espresso and add one big ice cube to cool. Whisk in 10 g cocoa powder and 3 tbsp Marsala or Amaretto until dissolved.
2. Separate 4 yolks from whites. Put 4 yolks in a large bowl, 2 whites in a medium bowl, save the last 2 whites for another meal.
3. Whisk the 4 egg yolks with 100 g sugar using a hand electric whisk until pale, thick and doubled in volume (about 3-4 min).
4. Add 500 g mascarpone and a pinch of salt. Whisk on low speed until just smooth. Don't overwork it.
5. In a separate bowl, whip 200 ml heavy cream to soft peaks with the hand whisk.
6. In another clean bowl, whip 2 egg whites to stiff peaks.
7. Fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture with a spatula. Then gently fold in the egg whites in two additions. Keep it airy.
8. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the coffee mixture — about 1 second per side. Don't soak them or they'll collapse.
9. Line the bottom of a ~20×25 cm dish with a single layer of dipped ladyfingers. Spread half the cream over them. Dust with 10 g cocoa powder. Repeat: another layer of dipped ladyfingers, then the remaining cream.
10. Sift remaining 10 g cocoa powder over the top. Cover with cling film and refrigerate at least 6 hours, ideally overnight.
