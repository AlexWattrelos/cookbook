Pot-au-Feu

Servings: 4

SHOPPING LIST

Veggies:
2 large carrots (~200 g)
2 leeks
2 turnips (~150 g)
1 small celeriac (~150 g)
½ small savoy cabbage (~200 g)
4 medium waxy potatoes (~400 g)
2 onions
4 sprigs fresh thyme
2 bay leaves
1 large bunch flat-leaf parsley (~40 g total)

Bread:
4–6 thick slices rustic bread (sourdough or country loaf)

Fridge items:
2 tbsp capers
6 cornichons

Meat / Fish:
600 g beef chuck (paleron)
600 g beef shank (gîte), bone-in
4 marrow bones (~5 cm pieces, ask butcher to cut)

Anything else:
4 whole cloves
2 cloves garlic
Dijon mustard (to serve)
Coarse sea salt (to serve)
1 tbsp red wine vinegar
1 tsp fish sauce
80 ml olive oil

INSTRUCTIONS - START 3.5h before

1. Soak 4 marrow bones in cold salted water for at least 2 hours (or overnight in the fridge) to draw out blood for cleaner marrow. Drain and rinse before using.

2. Place 600 g beef chuck and 600 g beef shank in a large pot. Cover with ~3 L cold water. Bring to a boil over high heat. Skim off all foam thoroughly for 5–10 min until broth stays clear.

3. Peel 2 onions, halve them, and stud each half with 1 whole clove (4 cloves total). Add to the pot along with 4 sprigs thyme, 2 bay leaves, 6 stems parsley (tied together), and 1 tsp black peppercorns. Season with 1 tbsp salt. Reduce to the gentlest possible simmer — only the occasional bubble breaking the surface. Partially cover. Cook 2.5 hours.

4. While meat simmers, prep the vegetables:
   - Peel 2 carrots and cut into ~8 cm lengths.
   - Peel 2 turnips (~150 g) and quarter them.
   - Peel 1 small celeriac (~150 g) and cut into large wedges.
   - Peel 4 medium potatoes (~400 g) and halve them.
   - Halve ½ small savoy cabbage (~200 g), trimming the core but leaving a thin bit attached to hold each piece together. Blanch in boiling salted water for 3 min, drain, and set aside in a bowl.
   - Trim 2 leeks, halve lengthwise, and wash well. Tie into 1 bundle with kitchen string. Set aside in the bowl with the cabbage.

5. Sauce verte: pick leaves from 30 g parsley. Add 30 g parsley leaves, 6 cornichons, 2 tbsp capers, 1 tsp fish sauce, and 1 tbsp red wine vinegar to a blender. Blend until roughly chopped. Add 80 ml olive oil and blend briefly until combined but still slightly chunky. Season with pepper. Transfer to a bowl and set aside.

6. After 2.5 hours, add the carrots, turnips, celeriac wedges, and potatoes to the pot. Simmer 20 min.

7. Add the leek bundle and blanched cabbage. Simmer another 20–25 min until all vegetables are tender.

8. While the pot finishes: place 4 marrow bones cut-side up on a hot grill. Grill 15–20 min until marrow is bubbling, soft, and slightly golden on top. Sprinkle with coarse sea salt only after removing from grill.

9. Grill 4–6 thick slices rustic bread until charred on both sides. Rub each slice immediately with 1 halved clove of garlic (2 cloves total, halved).

10. Remove meat and slice against the grain into thick slices. Arrange on a warm platter with vegetables and grilled marrow bones. Serve grilled garlic bread alongside for spreading marrow. Strain broth and serve in bowls. Set out sauce verte, Dijon mustard, and coarse sea salt at the table.
