Roasted Salmon Quinoa Bowls with Avocado, Radishes & Cherry Tomatoes

Serves: 4
Prep: 20 min | Cook: 10 min | Total: ~30 min

INGREDIENTS

Salmon & Bowls
- 4 skinless salmon fillets (about 170 g each)
- 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 170 g quinoa (uncooked), cooked per pack instructions and cooled
- Fine salt & freshly ground black pepper
- 150 g radishes, thinly sliced
- 300 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 avocados, pitted and diced
- Small handful chopped fresh herbs (chives, basil, tarragon, or parsley), optional
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges

Soy–Sesame Vinaigrette (generous for 4 bowls)
- 80 ml olive oil
- 40 ml soy sauce
- 30 ml rice vinegar (apple cider or red wine also fine)
- 10 ml toasted sesame oil
- 2 small garlic cloves, finely grated (or ½ tsp garlic powder)
- Pinch chilli flakes or black pepper
- 20–30 ml water, to loosen to taste

METHOD
1. Heat oven to 220°C (fan 200°C). Line a large baking tray with foil.
2. Oil & season: Brush the foil with 15 ml olive oil. Pat salmon dry, season with ~½ tsp salt and pepper, place on the tray, and drizzle with 15 ml olive oil.
3. Roast: Bake 8–10 min, until just cooked through.
4. Quinoa: Toss the cooked quinoa with the remaining 30 ml olive oil and a pinch of salt.
5. Vinaigrette: In a jar, combine olive oil, soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, and chilli/pepper. Shake. Add 20–30 ml water to reach a pourable, mellow taste.
6. Build bowls: Divide quinoa among 4 bowls. Top with salmon, radishes, cherry tomatoes, and avocado. Lightly salt the veg and avocado. Add herbs if using.
7. Serve: Drizzle generously with the vinaigrette and add lemon wedges on the side.
