White-Chocolate Cheesecake

Crust
- 250 g Speculoos cookies (or any spiced crisp cookie)
- 75 g unsalted butter, melted

Filling
- 730 g cream cheese (Philadelphia-style) — room temperature
- 220 ml thick crème fraîche
- 400 g white baking chocolate, chopped
- 90 g sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 1 lime
- 100 ml raspberry purée or sauce

Equipment
- 23 cm springform pan
- Baking parchment

1. Take 730 g Philadelphia out of the fridge and let it warm up
2. Prepare the crust
   - Crush 250 g speculoos (don't grind to dust). Thermomix: 6 s, speed 5.
   - Mix in 75 g melted butter (600 W, 1 min 50) until every crumb is coated.
   - Press the mixture firmly into the base of the pan. Chill for 45 min.
3. Make the filling
   - Gently melt 400 g white chocolate with the 220 ml crème fraîche over low heat, stirring until smooth. Thermomix: 10 min, 50 °C
   - In a large bowl, beat 730 g cream cheese, one full lime juice with 90 g sugar until creamy.
   - Beat in gently 3 eggs one at a time.
   - Stir in the melted white-chocolate mixture until the batter is silky - do not add air bubbles.
4. Assemble
   - Line the inner sides of the pan with a strip of baking parchment (stick it in place with water).
   - Swirl the purée over the top for a marbled look.
5. Bake
   - Add in a cup of water in a bowl in your oven and bake at 155 °C (conventional oven) for 60 min. Center of the cake should read >66 °C. If not, let it run another 10 mins and recheck.
   - Turn the oven off, open the door slightly, and let the cake cool inside for another 90 min (prevents cracks).
6. Chill & serve
   - Refrigerate for at least 24 h
   - Cheesecake improves after 2–3 days and keeps well if kept cold and covered.
