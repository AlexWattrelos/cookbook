RASPBERRY TART (30 cm / 12-inch) — older version, Aug 2025

YIELD 1 tart - serves 8
TIMELINE 30 min active • 1 h chilling • 30 min baking • 30 min assembly

SHOPPING LIST / INGREDIENTS
- 90 g unsalted butter
- 230 g all-purpose (plain) flour
- 60 g caster (super-fine) sugar
- 60 g sugar
- 8 g vanilla sugar ≈ 1 tsp vanilla paste
- 375 ml whole milk
- 4 large eggs
- 500 g fresh raspberries
- 2 tbsp seedless raspberry or apricot jam

1 SHORTCRUST PASTRY
1. 90 g butter + 195 g flour → 30 s / speed 5 (crumbly).
2. 60 g caster sugar + 1 egg + pinch salt + pinch fleur de sel → 10 s / speed 5.
3. Knead 1 min 30 s / Dough-mode.
4. Flatten, wrap, chill 1 h.

2 CRÈME PÂTISSIÈRE
1. 375 g milk + 35 g flour + 3 eggs + 8 g vanilla sugar + 60 g sugar → into bowl.
2. Cook 7 min / 90 °C / speed 4 (thickens, should reach final temp between 82 and 87 °C).
3. Emulsify 6 s / speed 8.
4. Plastic wrap on surface, chill completely.

3 BLIND-BAKING THE SHELL
1. Roll pastry (≈ 3 mm) and line 30 cm tin.
2. Freeze 5 min.
3. Dock, parchment + weights.
4. Freeze 5 min.
5. Bake 15 min at 180 °C.
6. Remove weights; bake 10–12 min more until golden.
7. (Optional) Brush hot shell with beaten yolk or thin melted chocolate; 1–2 min extra.
8. Cool completely.

4 ASSEMBLY
1. Fill cooled shell with all pastry cream
2. Top with 500 g raspberries, upright and snug.
3. Warm 2 tbsp jam + 1 tsp water; brush berries for shine.
4. Chill 20–30 min; serve within 8 h for maximum crispness.

MAKE-AHEAD
- Unbaked dough up to 72 h, refrigerated. Take it out of the fridge 20 mins before rolling
- Pastry cream up to 48 h, refrigerated.
- Baked shell up to 24 h, airtight, room-temp.
- Assembled tart best eaten same day (crust softens after ≈ 8 h).
