MOULES À LA CRÈME (WITH CHARLOTTE, WHITE WINE, CRÈME FRAÎCHE, GARLIC)

Serves: 3–4 (as a main) or 5–6 (as a starter)
Time: ~20 minutes

INGREDIENTS
- 2 kg fresh mussels (cleaned, debearded)
- 2 2/3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 2/3 large shallots (or 5–6 small), finely chopped (charlotte)
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 267 ml dry white wine
- 267 g crème fraîche (full-fat)
- 2 2/3 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 1 1/3 tbsp lemon juice (optional, for brightness)
- 2/3 tsp black pepper
- 2/3 tsp salt (optional; add only after tasting)
- 1 generous pinch cayenne pepper or Espelette (optional)

EQUIPMENT
- 1 large pot with lid
- 1 colander

INSTRUCTIONS
1) 2 kg fresh mussels: Rinse 2 kg fresh mussels under cold water, scrub if needed, pull off any beards, and discard any that are cracked or won't close when tapped.
2) 2 2/3 tbsp unsalted butter: Melt 2 2/3 tbsp unsalted butter in a large pot over medium heat.
3) 2 2/3 large shallots: Add 2 2/3 large shallots and cook 3–4 minutes until soft and translucent (don't brown).
4) 4 cloves garlic: Add 4 cloves garlic and cook 30–45 seconds until fragrant.
5) 267 ml dry white wine: Pour in 267 ml dry white wine and bring to a lively simmer for 1 minute.
6) 2 kg fresh mussels: Add 2 kg fresh mussels, cover with the lid, and steam 4–6 minutes, shaking the pot once or twice, until most have opened.
7) 2/3 tsp black pepper: Add 2/3 tsp black pepper (and 1 generous pinch cayenne pepper if using). Stir gently.
8) 267 g crème fraîche: Lower the heat to medium-low and stir in 267 g crème fraîche until the sauce is smooth. Simmer 1–2 minutes (do not boil hard).
9) 1 1/3 tbsp lemon juice: Stir in 1 1/3 tbsp lemon juice (optional). Taste the sauce, then add 2/3 tsp salt only if needed (mussels can be salty).
10) 2 2/3 tbsp chopped fresh parsley: Turn off the heat and stir in 2 2/3 tbsp chopped fresh parsley.
11) Serving: Ladle into bowls with plenty of sauce and serve immediately (great with fries, crusty bread, or pasta).

NOTES / TIPS
- 2 kg fresh mussels: Discard any mussels that stay closed after cooking.
- 267 ml dry white wine: Use a crisp wine (Muscadet, Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Grigio, Chasselas).
- 267 g crème fraîche: Full-fat crème fraîche is best to prevent splitting.
