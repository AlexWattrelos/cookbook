TM7-ONLY BEURRE BLANC FOR 1 kg TURBOT

Yield: ~350 ml — for ~1 kg turbot

SHOPPING LIST

Produce
- 80 g shallots

Dairy
- 250 g unsalted butter (very cold)

Pantry
- 120 ml dry white wine
- 30 ml white wine vinegar

INSTRUCTIONS

1) Cut 250 g unsalted butter into 1–2 cm cubes. Keep 250 g unsalted butter in the fridge until needed.
2) Add 80 g shallots. TM7: Chop 5 sec / speed 6. Scrape down.
3) Add 120 ml dry white wine + 30 ml white wine vinegar. TM7: Cook 12–16 min / 100°C / speed 1, no measuring cup, until reduced to about 2–3 tbsp of syrupy liquid.
4) Let the reduction cool slightly in the bowl 2 min (this helps prevent splitting).
5) TM7: Set to 70°C / speed 3 (no time set yet).
6) Stop → open lid → add 30–40 g of the cold 250 g unsalted butter cubes → close lid → run 10–15 sec / 70°C / speed 3.
7) Repeat Step 6, adding 30–40 g more cold 250 g unsalted butter each time, until all 250 g unsalted butter is fully emulsified and the sauce is glossy.
8) If it looks thin, TM7: Cook 1–2 min / 70°C / speed 3. If it looks too thick, Stop → open lid → add 10–20 ml cold water → close lid → run 10 sec / 70°C / speed 3.
9) Season to taste with salt and white pepper. Serve immediately, or keep warm: TM7: 60°C / speed 1 for up to 20 min (do not overheat or boil).

OPTIONAL: Stop → open lid → add 1 tsp finely chopped tarragon → close lid → mix 5 sec / speed 2.
